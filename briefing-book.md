### Key Scientific Facts
- AB-300 = deuterated (R)-dimoxamine, 5-HT2A/2C partial agonist, non-hallucinogenic
- Parent compound tested in 200+ patients by Bristol-Myers in 1970s
- PD Apathy affects 40-70% of Parkinson's patients, NO FDA-approved treatment
- Phase 1B is signal-seeking (NOT confirmatory)
- Dosing: 4 days on / 3 days off regimen (BID, 25-75mg range)
- Competitor: IR Labs (IRL757) in European PD apathy trial
- Key risk: 5-HT2B receptor activity -- cardiac valvulopathy monitoring required

## INTERPRETATION RULES FOR SARA

1. **NEVER upgrade financial status.** "Budget model extends to 2027" =/= "funded through 2027." "Target raise of $8M" =/= "$8M secured." Only explicit "signed/wired/committed/closed" = confirmed.

2. **NEVER upgrade partnership status.** First meeting = EXPLORATORY. Diligence = IN PROGRESS. Only signed term sheets = COMMITTED.

3. **NEVER upgrade clinical status.** "Planning Phase 1B" =/= "Phase 1B initiated." "Pre-IND preparation" =/= "FDA feedback received."

4. **Use MJFF, not "Fox Foundation."** Full name: Michael J. Fox Foundation.

5. **Distinguish internal vs investor documents.** Internal budgets track actual spending. Investor-facing models project future needs. Different purposes, different numbers.

6. **No attribution to individuals.** Use "the team discussed," "progress was reported on." Never "Ken said" or "Shlomi mentioned."

7. **Geopolitical sensitivity.** Report business impact of Israel conflict (delayed meetings, fundraising headwinds) without political commentary.

8. **Clinical trial sites in Israel:** Ichilov (primary), Sheba Tel Hashomer, Hadassah, Shamir, potential Bellinson/Rambam. CRO: Galilee CBR.

9. **Manufacturing chain:** API synthesis at Eurofins Advinus (India) -> capsule formulation at Recipharm (India/Israel) -> clinical sites in Israel/Austria.

10. **When in doubt, DOWNGRADE confidence.** A false negative (missing a win) is far less harmful than a false positive (reporting something achieved that hasn't happened).
